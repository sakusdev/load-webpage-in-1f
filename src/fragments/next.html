First-flight contract

Nothing below the fold may delay the first paint.

01 / DOCUMENTOne request.

The first viewport ships as one self-contained document: markup, critical CSS, and only the tiny loader needed to unlock deferred work.

02 / PAINTZero blockers.

No external stylesheet, framework runtime, web font, analytics SDK, or hero image stands between the response and the first paint.

03 / DEFERAfter frame one.

The next section is not requested until two animation frames have elapsed. Deep content waits until it approaches the viewport.
